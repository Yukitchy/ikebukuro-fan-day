Two hours at a manga artist's desk

MANGA DOJO Tokyo is a small manga school a few minutes from the station. The teachers are working manga artists, an interpreter sits in the room, and the school hands you the real tools: G-pens, ink, screen tone and manga manuscript paper. Nicholas leaves with his own character and a four panel comic.

The teachers
Working manga artists. The school's head instructor, Kazuo Maekawa, drew the Ace Attorney manga series.
The tools
G-pens, ink, screen tone and official manuscript paper are on the desk. Nothing to bring.
Who it suits
Beginners are welcome and the school takes children from age five. Adults draw at the same table.

Both the 13:00 and the 15:15 classes are open on December 20. The school recommends 13:00 for families; 15:15 is its advanced class. Classes start exactly on time, so we arrive five minutes early.

Workshop first, then the shops

Draw at 13:00, shop until 18:30

Start with the two hour manga workshop while everyone is fresh, then the shopping is the reward. We meet at the school and the day ends at the station side, close to the trains for your dinner reservation. The school recommends the 13:00 class for families.

This works for us
  1. 13:00
    MANGA DOJO Tokyo workshopWe meet at the school door at 12:55. Two hours of drawing manga with a working manga artist, English interpretation included. Nicholas draws his own character and takes the pages home.
  2. 15:00
    Walk to Sunshine CityAbout 8 minutes on foot along Sunshine 60 Street.
  3. 15:10
    Mugiwara Store and Pokémon Center Mega TokyoSunshine City, Alpa 2F. The official One Piece store and Japan's largest Pokémon Center are on the same floor. Open to 20:00 on Sunday.
  4. 16:10
    Gashapon Department Store and the One Piece Card Game shopWorld Import Mart 3F, same building. Over 3,000 capsule toy machines, then Bandai Namco Cross Store with the official One Piece Card Game shop and Tamashii Nations figures. Open to 21:00.
  5. 17:00
    BOOKOFF Sunshine 60 StreetTwo floors of second hand manga, figures, anime goods and Pokémon cards. One Piece volumes in Japanese are everywhere. Open to 22:00.
  6. 17:40
    Animate Ikebukuro flagshipNine floors, one of the biggest anime shops in Japan. New manga, figures and limited goods across every series. Open to 21:00.
  7. 18:15
    JUMP SHOP Ikebukuro PARCOB2F of PARCO, right at the East Exit. The official Weekly Shonen Jump store: One Piece next to Naruto and Dragon Ball, with items sold nowhere else. Open to 21:00.
  8. 18:30
    DoneIkebukuro Station East Exit. Dinner at 20:30 is yours.

Everything is on foot. Workshop → Sunshine City about 8 min. Sunshine City → Animate about 5 min. Animate → PARCO about 5 min. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Good for
A family that wants the hands-on part done first and a relaxed shopping run after.
Keep in mind
The 13:00 class is open on December 20 and is the one the school recommends for families. Shopping bags get heavy by the end, so the last two stops are short. Most stores have a tax free counter, so keep your passports with you.

MANGA DOJO Tokyo, 13:00

We meet at the school itself at 13:00: MANGA DOJO Tokyo, Sansho Building 602, 1-31-6 Higashi-Ikebukuro, Toshima-ku. It is 5 minutes on foot from the East Exit of Ikebukuro Station and 2 minutes from Animate. The afternoon ends at the station side at 18:30.

MANGA DOJO Tokyo, Sansho Building 602, 1-31-6 Higashi-Ikebukuro, Toshima-ku, Tokyo 170-0013 ↗

Ikebukuro is on the JR Yamanote line and three subway lines. From most of central Tokyo it is one train. Please arrive by 12:55; the class starts exactly on time.
