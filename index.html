Tokyo, Sunday Dec 20

Ikebukuro for One Piece and Pokémon fans

Everything below is within a 10 minute walk of Ikebukuro Station's east side. Hours are the stores' Sunday hours, checked on their official sites in October 2026.

  • One Piece official store
  • Pokémon Center
  • Used manga
  • Figures and capsule toys

Inside Sunshine City

One big complex, 8 minutes from the station. The One Piece store and the Pokémon Center are on the same floor, so this is the easiest stop of the day.

A few tips. Most stores have a tax free counter for purchases over 5,000 yen, so keep your passports with you. Capsule toy machines are coin only. Hours can change around the holidays, so the official links above are the final word on the day.
